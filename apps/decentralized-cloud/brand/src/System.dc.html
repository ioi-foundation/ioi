IOI Display is unicase and carries no digits and no period, so it sets the wordmark and nothing else — never a price, never a timestamp, never a heading with a number in it.
Evidence states — the one component the product cannot do without
live_evidence simulated_control_plane advisory_only candidate_source_unavailable expired not wired — waiting on M15.3
Green is earned. Only a candidate the daemon returned with evidence_mode: live_evidence may wear it. A simulator candidate is grey, is labelled in words, and never counts toward a fee.
Absence is a state. A source with no adapter or no credential says so, in the daemon's own words, in the row where a price would have been. An invented price is the one unrecoverable mistake this product can make.
Unwired is visible. Write surfaces designed ahead of the kernel carry the last chip and cannot be submitted. A stub the reader cannot tell from truth is refused.
