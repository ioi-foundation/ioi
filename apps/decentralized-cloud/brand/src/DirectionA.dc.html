Direction A — the expiring dot
decentralizedcloud
The case. The one character in the name that IOI Display cannot set is the period — so the period becomes the mark. The dot is a price; the open ring is the window it is good for; the gap is that it expires. Nothing else in the product means anything different by a dot.
The tradeoff. A dot is the most common shape in software. This wins on meaning and on the 16px tab, and loses on standing out in a row of logos.
