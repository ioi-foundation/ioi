The case. The one character in the name that IOI Display cannot set is the period, so the period is what gets drawn. It carries the product's actual thesis rather than a picture of infrastructure: the dot is a price, the arc is the window that price is good for, and the missing hundred degrees are the part of the window already spent.
The tradeoff. A dot in a ring is well-trodden ground in software, and the arc has to be dropped below 24px, so the idea does not survive all the way down to a favicon — only the dot does. It earns its place on meaning and on the wordmark, not on novelty of shape.
