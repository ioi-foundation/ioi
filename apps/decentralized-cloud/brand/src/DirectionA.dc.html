The case. The one character in the name that IOI Display cannot set is the period, so the period is what gets drawn. It carries the product's actual thesis rather than a picture of infrastructure: the dot is a price, the arc is the window that price is good for, and the 104 degrees sliced out of it are the part of the window already spent.
The outcome. Rejected as a logo and kept as a component. Independent review found it read as a loading spinner at 24px and as a stray letterform at 16px, and the construction that was meant to rescue it — terminals cut flat and parallel — was never what the SVG drew: butt caps cut along the radius, so the two faces sat 104° apart. The ring is now the freshness dial beside a quote, where being a progress ring is honest, and the identity is wordmark-led.
