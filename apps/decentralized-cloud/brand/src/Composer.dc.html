what comes back
A receipt chain, not a status string. The job is complete only when the chain is: a job whose provider vanished mid-run is complete after failover or after a receipted refusal, and not before.
what this surface never owns
No session plane, no credential vault, no provider integration, no placement scorer, no receipt format of its own. wallet.network authorizes, Hypervisor places and executes, Agentgres records what ran and what it cost. This page composes those four and adds none.
