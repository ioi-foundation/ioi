The dot
One quarter of the cap height, rounded up to the nearest pixel. On this sheet: 11px at 64, 7px at 40, 5px at 28, 4px at 22. Cap height is 0.70 em in this face, measured off the rendered glyph.
Clear space
One cap height on every side, measured from the outermost ink — 45px around the 64px lockup above. Nothing sits inside it, the IOI endorsement line included.
Minimum size
Stacked to 220px wide, which puts the cap at 11px. Horizontal to 400px. Below 220px the icon alone. Never set the wordmark in another face and never track it past 0.01em.
