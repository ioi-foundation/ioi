The idea. The wordmark's face has no period, so the period is drawn — and the drawn period becomes the mark. The dot is a price; the ring around it is the window that price is good for; the missing hundred degrees are the part of the window already spent.
The cost. A dot inside a ring is one of the most occupied shapes in software, and it has to hold a visible gap between dot and ring at 16px or it collapses into a blob.
