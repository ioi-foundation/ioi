# M11 — Useful Same-System Distributed Work And Embodied Pre-Live Proof

Prove that one bounded System distributes useful *complementary* work across its admitted nodes rather than replicas, and prove the native embodied graph through non-live promotion stages only.

## Why this is next

`sequence.v1.json` places M11 at canonical build step 11 with `depends_on: ["M10"]`, so the predecessor's two-failure-domain continuity supplies the surviving-System substrate that complementary role placement is layered onto. M11 adds what continuity alone does not establish: that differently-shaped work can be placed, leased, fenced, and reconciled inside one `system_id`, and that an exact embodied graph can be compiled and exercised under simulation, SIL, HIL, and shadow with no live actuator path. It closes claim level `P4` and feeds M12, whose `depends_on` set includes M11.

## In scope

- `M11.1` bind GoalRun, RoleTopology, work, resource/capability offers, and RuntimeAssignments to the stable System and admitted memberships;
- `M11.2` implement allocation leases, backpressure, shared-state watermarks, coordination epochs, fenced reassignment, rebalance, and rejoin;
- `M11.3` inject worker, coordinator, node, link, and partition failure during useful work;
- `M11.4` reconcile duplicate and ambiguous effects while showing which work continues, pauses, reassigns, or fails closed;
- `M11.5` freeze and register graph/profile/stream/action/supervisor/activation/reservation/assurance contracts before implementation;
- `M11.5a` admit `EmbodiedUnit` and `EmbodiedResourceGroup` definitions for individual sensors, actuators, controllers, robots, drones, and logical or physical groups without requiring every member to run HypervisorOS;
- `M11.6` compile one exact `EmbodiedRuntimeGraphManifest` across `micro`, `edge`, and `site` profiles without changing graph semantics or authority;
- `M11.7` prepare and validate resources, stream QoS/time/criticality, supervisor readiness, and single-writer bindings before transactional activation;
- `M11.8` run simulation, SIL, HIL, and shadow promotion with proposal-only action chunks, deterministic motion, independent local veto, recovery, and emergency stop;
- `M11.9` exercise epoch-fenced allocation and `SpacetimeReservationLease` across units while local safety remains local;
- `M11.10` inject deadline, freshness, clock, liveliness, GPU/autonomy, uncertainty-overlap, reboot/restore, lost offline holdover, coordinator, partition, reservation, stale allocation, takeover, and ambiguous-effect faults;
- old-to-current name/owner migration across the embodied contract families, with compatibility and migration rules;
- the non-live operator/safety journey and safety case over simulation/SIL/HIL/shadow, promotion, and rollback receipts;
- pulled product surfaces Systems, Work, Operations, Provenance, and Foundry, plus the *conditional* Embodied Systems non-live operational-journey record, which M11 owns **only when its registration is pulled**;
- positive, adversarial, stale-state, substitution, fault, recovery, product/operator-state, and retained-evidence planning for the bounded claim.

## Out of scope

- Live actuator invocation, live physical mission, or any asserted E1+ promotion — a separately approved conditional future carried by `live-embodied-promotion.v1.json`.
- Cross-System and cross-sovereign work: AIIP federation belongs to M12, two-sovereign operation to M13; consensus, sovereign independence, and independent-party claims belong to neither.
- Generic certification of hardware, a system, or a supplier.
- Any exit or activation owned by a neighbouring stage's aggregate.

## Exit proof

`sequence.v1.json` binds the M11 exit gate to `m11-selected-profile-exit-proof` (`kind: aggregate_exit`). For that aggregate to be verifiable, all of the following must hold and be retained as evidence:

- complementary roles complete useful work across nodes under unchanged authority;
- placement never grants authority;
- one active actuator writer, safe local degrade/stop, fenced takeover, and rejoin reconciliation hold in the declared non-live stages;
- no model, cloud, chain, remote wallet, or stale telemetry becomes the final control veto or emergency stop;
- simulation/SIL/HIL/shadow evidence remains labeled at its actual stage;
- each unconditional child has closed its own literal exit — `M11_USEFUL_SAME_SYSTEM_DISTRIBUTION_EXIT=0`, `M11_EMBODIED_NONLIVE_GRAPH_PROOF_EXIT=0`, `M11_EMBODIED_CONTRACT_ALIGNMENT_EXIT=0`, `M11_FOUNDRY_PROMOTION_SAFETY_PRODUCT_EXIT=0` — and the conditional child's `M11_EMBODIED_SYSTEMS_NONLIVE_JOURNEY_EXIT=0` when and only when its activation gate is selected;
- retained evidence contains exactly one literal `M11_SELECTED_PROFILE_EXIT_PROOF_EXIT=0`.

The M11 aggregate may join only the Embodied Systems non-live literal exit; live actuator/E1+ promotion remains a separately approved conditional future.

## Blocks advancement

- Unmet `depends_on: ["M10"]`, or any unclosed unconditional child in the aggregate's disposition set.
- The stage's declared external gate — the relevant embodied/safety contract freeze — while it remains prose rather than an approved, named gate binding.
- The per-record `applicable-pg-profile-gates` external gate, and `PG-5.1` (resolve and cryptographically verify referenced deployment, stream, timing, ODD, proof-test, authority, writer, and sensor evidence rather than validating supplied bindings only), defined in `internal-docs/implementation/proof-gates/mechanism-gate-registry.md`.
- Any embodied contract implementation depends on that has not been frozen and registered under `M11.5`; or an unknown alias or stale record blocking the old-to-current migration.
- A missing supervisor, missing lease, missing safety case, or unsafe candidate anywhere in the promotion path.
- Any unresolved entry under "Canon gaps" that an in-scope obligation depends on.

## Nonclaims

- No live physical mission, no generic certification, no cross-system federation.
- Exactly one `system_id`; no AIIP or independent-party claim.
- Node count and placement grant no authority, consensus, sovereign independence, or federation.
- Simulation/SIL/HIL/shadow evidence, or a planned record, proves no live actuator path and no E1+ claim; all physical evidence stays labeled at its actual non-live promotion stage.
- No remote final veto: no model, cloud, chain, remote wallet, or stale telemetry may become the local safety loop or emergency stop.
- This stage module closes no work item, stage, runtime capability, application, or public claim.
