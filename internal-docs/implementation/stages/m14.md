# M14 — Connected/Secured Services And Demand-Gated L1

Add public shared-trust services, and authorize a sovereign L1 at most, only after their value and security economics are demonstrated as real.

## Why this is next

Per [`sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json), M14 depends on M13, whose aggregate exit establishes independently governed Systems doing useful positive-surplus work with portable exit (claim level `P5`). M14 adds the layer above that boundary: public service families carrying registry, rights, reputation, escrow, bond, dispute, adjudication, settlement, enrollment, and exit contracts, plus the measured demand and security economics that decide whether a sovereign L1 is authorized at all. It is the sole stage carrying claim level `P7`.

## In scope

- `M14.1` prove registry, rights, reputation, security service, escrow, bond, dispute, adjudication, settlement, enrollment, and exit contracts on a devnet or mature compatible settlement environment.
- `M14.2` separate `ioi_compatible`, `ioi_connected`, and `ioi_secured` postures and their exact services, terms, fees, and assurances.
- `M14.3` measure recurring unrelated external demand, willingness to pay or bear risk, provider/guardian/verifier supply, attack cost, security budget, regulatory burden, and compatible-settlement alternatives.
- `M14.4` prove value persists with token-price appreciation set to zero.
- `M14.5` authorize a sovereign L1 only when neutral ordering/security, performance, governance, or economics beats the compatible alternative.
- `M14.6` keep Work Credits, provider tokens, cash, worker payouts, bonds, and any native asset distinct.
- Own `connected-worker-capability-supply-and-hiring`, `decentralized-profile-admission-and-exit`, and `ecosystem-assurance-and-public-claim-estate` as M14 records; the connected-worker record owns external digital-worker discovery, capability hiring, lifecycle, and portable exit only.
- Keep `m14-service-family-owner-contract-and-product-surfaces` as the sole sas service registry/order/delivery/acceptance/payment/service-dispute-binding/suspension/exit journey owner.
- Preserve the valid no-L1 branch and all demand/mainnet nonclaims in every record.
- Plan positive, adversarial, stale-state, substitution, fault, recovery, product/operator-state, compatibility, migration, and retained-evidence obligations for each bounded claim.

## Out of scope

- Adjudication, appeal, and remedy mechanism: the M12 dispute kernel (`dispute-adjudication-remedy-kernel`) remains the sole owner; M14 records bind to it and never co-own it.
- The distinct M8 billing, local-worker, and neutrality exits (`managed-billing-work-credits-and-supplier-reconciliation`, `worker-training-and-local-marketplace-supply`, `marketplace-neutral-routing-contribution-accounting`): M14 depends on those exits and appends no scope to those earlier record IDs.
- PG-7.2 correlated failure injection across selected shared planes, owned by the `FUTURE`-stage record `m14-cross-plane-correlated-failure-injection`.
- Mainnet launch, native-asset issuance, and any embodied promotion (`P6`, `FUTURE`).
- Generic sas service orders inside the connected-worker record; worker-lifecycle co-ownership inside the service-family record.

## Exit proof

The stage exit gate is the `aggregate_exit` record `m14-selected-profile-aggregate-exit`, which must remain distinct from the demand, service, and L1-decision records it joins. For that record to close, all of the following must hold:

- at least three unrelated external organizations show recurring paid or risk-bearing demand across at least two public service families for a sustained declared period;
- modeled fees and bonded value cover the security/availability budget with a frozen safety margin;
- independent operators will supply the selected security, verification, availability, relay, or adjudication services;
- exit, dispute, and failure paths work before mainnet or native-asset commitment;
- retained evidence contains exactly one literal `M14_EXIT=0` bound to exact artifact bytes, and each joined record's own literal exit is satisfied in its own evidence.

## Blocks advancement

- The M13 dependency edge: `m13-selected-profile-aggregate-exit` must be closed, and the sequencer's external gates — repeated M13 evidence, later enterprise/embodied profile demand, and measured external demand sufficient to authorize optional L1 — must be met.
- Cross-stage record dependencies must be closed: `dispute-adjudication-remedy-kernel` (M12), and `managed-billing-work-credits-and-supplier-reconciliation`, `marketplace-neutral-routing-contribution-accounting`, `worker-training-and-local-marketplace-supply` (M8).
- Named external gates on the records: `applicable-pg-profile-gates`, `commercial-legal-assurance-demand-approval`, and, for the conditional profile, `selected-decentralized-profile`. The stage prerequisite is the full set of applicable production, commercial, legal, assurance, and economic gates, not any one of them.
- Applicable mechanism gates must be mapped and evidenced without redefinition: `PG-2.6` (transparency profile for the L1 decision), `PG-6A.2`, `PG-6A.3`, `PG-6B.3` (service-family payment, payout, bond/escrow movement), `PG-6D.1`, `PG-6D.2`, `PG-6D.3` (jurisdictional inputs, effectors, accountable-issuer legal claim).
- Any unresolved canon gap below, since the stage cannot resolve doctrine inside a work item.

## Nonclaims

- No compulsory settlement; no token demand inferred from L0 adoption; no generic model-call currency; no one token per worker, GoalRun, room, node, or DAS.
- The valid no-L1 branch remains available; planning and devnet work prove no demand, no mainnet, no native asset, and no recurring external cohort.
- Internal traffic, L0 adoption, System or receipt counts, and token speculation never qualify as demand; affiliates never substitute for unrelated organizations, and the declared period is never shortened.
- Connected service availability establishes neither demand nor L1 authorization.
- No mainnet, native-asset, generic safety, generic correctness, or stage-closure claim arises from a devnet or a plan.
- A legal-conformity assertion requires a separately authorized accountable issuer; the generated evaluator remains `not_determined`.
