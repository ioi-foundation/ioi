# M7 — Local Ontology And Action Semantics

Let each domain own versioned meaning and compile semantic intent into typed actions without semantics granting power.

## Why this is next

`sequence.v1.json` gives M7 one `depends_on` edge to M6; its work items also depend on
`m6-product-surface-and-typed-workspaces` and `m6-consequential-action-authority-receipt-unification`.
M6 establishes typed workspaces over real read models and one unified consequential-action
authority and receipt path. M7 adds the semantic layer those surfaces and actions read from —
immutable ontology versions, explicit crosswalks, provenance-bearing assertions,
content-addressed recipes, and action contracts. Admission requires M1–M6 verified so
semantic state is System-bound.

## In scope

- `M7.1` admit immutable ontology versions, overlays, compatibility, and deprecation contracts per namespace;
- `M7.2` admit explicit crosswalks and concrete challengeable mapping decisions with loss, ambiguity, scope, and reviewer evidence;
- `M7.3` admit provenance assertions with source, valid/transaction time, confidence, evidence, contradiction, and dispute state;
- `M7.4` split immutable DataRecipe and ConnectorMapping definitions from TransformationRun execution;
- `M7.5` freeze exact semantic component versions/hashes into packages and runs;
- `M7.6` admit OntologyActionContracts binding target objects and typed I/O to pre/postconditions, RuntimeToolContracts, authority, risk, idempotency, compensation, verifier, and receipt obligations;
- `M7.7` enforce external-fact/oracle evidence profiles at admission and consequence boundaries;
- `M7.8` render mapping uncertainty and disputes without pretending one global ontology exists.
- M7 owns the Studio, Ontology, and Data operational-journey records.
- Journeys: Define/version/map/act/dispute/migrate; Source→test/sync/transform/replay/recover/egress; Data truth/replay/restart; Design→validate/simulate→proposal/handoff; shared action/final-invoker mechanism plus minimum proof projections.
- Positive, adversarial, stale-state, substitution, fault, recovery, product/operator-state, compatibility, migration, and retained-evidence planning for each bounded claim.
- Pulled product surfaces: Ontology, Data, Studio, Provenance, Domain/extension interfaces, Developer Console federation configuration.

## Out of scope

- The product surface compiler, typed workspaces, and the unified consequential-action authority/receipt mechanism — M6.
- Institutional learning boundary, training/distillation eligibility, Foundry model supply, provider exit, and campaign improvement — M8.
- Federated ontology resolution, cross-System semantic cooperation, AIIP-mediated exchange — M12/M13; marketplace ontology-pack exchange and public economics — M14; Governance and Provenance surface ownership, contract-pulled from M1 onward rather than established here.
- Fixture truth, stale recipe resolution, credential leak, unreceipted egress, replay divergence, mutable admitted versions, uncertain-mapping concealment, oracle self-resolution, client-asserted authority, copied Workshop/Module surfaces as authority, and direct genesis or semantic writes from Studio.

## Exit proof

Bound to `m7-semantic-definition-action-plane`, whose retained evidence must contain exactly
one literal `M7_SEMANTIC_DEFINITION_ACTION_PLANE_EXIT=0`. The aggregate may join the Studio,
Ontology, and Data literal exits only after the semantic/data mechanism exits:
`M7_DATA_TRANSFORMATION_PROVENANCE_REPLAY_EXIT=0` and
`M7_ONTOLOGY_ACTION_FINAL_INVOKER_PRODUCT_EXIT=0`, then `M7_ONTOLOGY_OPERATIONAL_JOURNEY_EXIT=0`,
`M7_DATA_OPERATIONAL_JOURNEY_EXIT=0`, and `M7_STUDIO_OPERATIONAL_JOURNEY_EXIT=0`.

For the stage to be declared complete, all of the following must be true:

- the same signed package or recipe cannot silently execute against a later semantic head;
- changed mapping or ontology content requires a successor definition and explicit resolution;
- semantic compatibility, discovery, or mapping never grants capability, authority, truth, or acceptance;
- contradictory and uncertain assertions remain inspectable.

## Blocks advancement

- `m6-product-surface-and-typed-workspaces` and `m6-consequential-action-authority-receipt-unification` must reach their own verified state; M7 records name them as dependencies.
- Any child literal exit missing; an aggregate assembled from task/process exit codes, UI state, or plans instead of retained literals; a missing application child in the M7 evidence manifest; a mutable admitted version; an unguarded action; or replay divergence.
- The unregistered semantic contract families under **Canon gaps**: without a registered schema and generated projection there is no `R-CONTRACT` artifact to bind evidence to.
- Applicable `PG-*` gates: none declared on the M7 records; any gate later mapped here must be satisfied without redefining it.

## Nonclaims

- No globally canonical ontology and no cross-System cooperation; semantic compatibility is not correctness, authority, acceptance, or stage closure.
- Admission records that a domain admitted an assertion or decision, and a transformation receipt attests boundary facts only; neither makes the assertion universally true or correct.
- A permitted policy-bound view alone is not training consent.

## Canon gaps

- **Unregistered semantic contract families.** `docs/architecture/foundations/common-objects-and-envelopes.md` specifies `DomainOntologyEnvelope`, `OntologyAssertionEnvelope`, `OntologyMappingEnvelope`, `OntologyActionContractEnvelope`, `DataRecipeEnvelope`, `ConnectorMappingEnvelope`, `TransformationRunEnvelope`, and `PolicyBoundDataViewEnvelope` as reviewed shapes only. None carry a `schema://` contract id, schema version, or entry in `docs/architecture/_meta/schemas/architecture-contract-registry.v1.json`; of the M7 families only `OracleEvidenceProfileEnvelope` v1 is registered. Owner to resolve: `docs/architecture/foundations/common-objects-and-envelopes.md`, with `docs/architecture/foundations/domain-ontologies-and-data-recipes.md` as semantic owner.
- **Semantic content-hash canonicalization.** Canon fixes an exact effect-hash canonicalization profile for authority subjects and declares undefined canonicalization inadmissible, but names no canonicalization or digest rule for `DataRecipeEnvelope.content_hash` and the `semantic_component_set_snapshot_ref` set hash that `M7.5` and the first exit clause depend on. Owner to resolve: `docs/architecture/foundations/common-objects-and-envelopes.md`.
- **Typed surface states for mapping uncertainty and dispute.** `docs/architecture/foundations/domain-ontologies-and-data-recipes.md` requires surfaces to expose mapping posture and unresolved disagreement, but `docs/architecture/components/hypervisor/core-clients-surfaces.md` supplies no typed state vocabulary for either alongside its existing empty/degraded/blocked/denied states, which `M7.8` needs. Owner to resolve: `docs/architecture/components/hypervisor/core-clients-surfaces.md`.
