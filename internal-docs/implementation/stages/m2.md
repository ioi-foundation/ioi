# M2 — Deployment, Membership, Fencing, And Transition Truth

Separate desired topology from observed membership and make every writer and effect transition reconstructable and fenced.

## Why this is next

The `depends_on` edge in [`sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json) binds M2 to M1, whose build step lets one immutable package instantiate a bounded System with a protected constitutional and lifecycle identity. That identity says which System exists; it does not say which node is admitted, which writer holds the current epoch, or which effect is still allowed to land. M2 adds that operational layer — separately admitted deployment/membership records, a daemon-generated fence at the final invoker, and reconstructable route, backup, activation, and cleanup truth. M3 declares `depends_on` M1 and M2, so the System-bound path downstream inherits whatever M2 bounds here.

## In scope

- `M2.1` admit deployment, durability, failure-domain, node-role, and failover profiles separately from node identity;
- `M2.2` implement membership proposal/admission, role lease, readiness, catch-up, watermark, state-root, drain, removal, and degraded-state records;
- `M2.3` bind one single-node active-writer epoch and transition commitment to the System;
- `M2.4` make active resource fences daemon-generated and impossible for a caller to author;
- `M2.5` bind selected final invokers to current membership, epoch, resource, grant, and effect facts;
- `M2.6` preserve lost/ambiguous suffix and reconciliation obligations rather than inferring success from timeout or restore;
- `M2.7` rebuild desired and observed projections from Agentgres-backed truth;
- `M2.8` implement immutable route bindings whose ownership, TLS, target, observed state, activation head, renewal, detach, and drift remain separate from environment ports;
- `M2.9` implement manifest-complete environment backups and staged restore through `HypervisorChangePlan`, including aggregate read-only target preflight, apply-time revalidation, per-secret disposition, full-byte verification, and destination-local credential re-resolution;
- `M2.10` make activation a forward-only compare-and-swap and persist resource cleanup obligations across parent deletion, provider loss, rollback, supersession, and ambiguous effects;
- own `project-discovery-startup-and-session-chain` and `storage-profile-repair-and-availability` as M2 mechanism records; later stages may aggregate their evidence but may not redefine their truth;
- require the existing M2 selected-profile aggregate to join both new M2 records; neither may remain outside its child graph;
- node identity, attestation, secret custody, temporal floor, and ready-before-proof refusal (`m2-node-attestation-identity-secret-readiness`); Agentgres replay/recovery plus the minimum topology-state proof (`m2-agentgres-replay-recovery-and-product-topology`);
- registering the M2 contract families in `docs/architecture/_meta/schemas/architecture-contract-registry.v1.json` with generated Rust/TypeScript projections and adversarial fixtures, as `R-CONTRACT` requires;
- pulled product surfaces: Systems topology preview, Operations member actions, Environments placement, Provenance transition evidence.

## Out of scope

- Automatic failure detection wired to an external continuity witness and any promotion authority derived from detection (`PG-4A.2`); fence verification mounted at every consequential estate PEP and multi-node no-dual-writer fault suites (`PG-4A.5`, `PG-4A.6`) — all mapped by M10 records.
- Deployment across independent failure domains, two-node continuity, and topology chaos/operator journeys.
- The generic pursuit/result seam and the direct non-System path, which M3 declares.
- Operational Systems/Operations/Environments/Provenance journeys owned by M9 records; M2 owns only the minimum topology-state projection.
- Outcome recovery: environment recovery is bounded to environment state.

## Exit proof

Stage exit is the aggregate exit gate named in [`sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json): `m2-selected-profile-exit-proof`, whose retained evidence must contain exactly one literal `M2_SELECTED_PROFILE_EXIT_PROOF_EXIT=0`, with every child literal closed under it — `M2_MEMBERSHIP_READINESS_PLANE_EXIT=0`, `M2_WRITER_FENCE_AND_LOST_SUFFIX_EXIT=0`, `M2_ROUTE_RESTORE_ACTIVATION_CLEANUP_EXIT=0`, `M2_NODE_ATTESTATION_IDENTITY_SECRET_READINESS_EXIT=0`, `M2_AGENTGRES_REPLAY_RECOVERY_PRODUCT_EXIT=0`, `PROJECT_STARTUP_SESSION_CHAIN_EXIT=0`, `STORAGE_REPAIR_AVAILABILITY_EXIT=0`. For that gate to close, all of the following must be true on the selected single-node profile:

- a stale, foreign, deposed, malformed, or caller-authored writer context reaches zero effectors;
- restart repairs projections without hiding fork, gap, tamper, collision, or unreadable legacy state;
- node placement cannot widen authority, finality, or System identity;
- desired topology and observed readiness remain visibly distinct; and
- incomplete backup manifests, stale restore preflights, failed or superseded candidates, and ambiguous cleanup outcomes cannot advance active state or disappear with their parent objects.

## Blocks advancement

- The M1 dependency edge and the `m1-selected-profile-exit-proof` dependency carried by six of the seven children.
- Any child left outside the aggregate's child graph, or a duplicated/missing child literal in the retained evidence.
- `PG-4A.1`, `PG-4A.3`, and `PG-4A.4` mapped without redefinition; their definitions stay in the mechanism-gate registry module.
- M2 contract families that remain unregistered in the architecture contract registry, leaving `R-CONTRACT` open for shapes this stage must fence on.
- Any unresolved item under "Canon gaps" that a child record depends on.

## Nonclaims

- Automated failover, leader election, consensus, or two-node continuity.
- No promotion, automatic failover, or useful-work distribution follows from the membership plane; node placement grants no product authority and closes no stage.
- Fencing is not consensus and creates no authority beyond wallet grants and sealed intents.
- Environment recovery does not imply outcome recovery; no M10 continuity, provider reliability, or stage-closure claim follows from route, restore, activation, or cleanup work.
- The selected profile is single-node only.
