# M1 — Bounded-System Constitutional Core

Let one immutable package instantiate a real bounded System with a protected constitutional and lifecycle identity.
Inherits [`program/rules.md`](../program/rules.md); sequenced by [`program/sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json).

A valid `single_authority` deployment with no distributed consensus is still a bounded intelligent-blockchain state
machine when its constitution, admitted transitions, authority, receipts, replay, lifecycle, and effect boundaries are
real. Consensus, replication, and public settlement are profile choices, not the definition of boundedness.

## Why this is next

The single `depends_on` edge is `M0`, which freezes what may be claimed and locks the program-control model before any
product stage advances. M1 spends that lock on canonical build step 1: a durable `system://` identity with a
constitution, an ordered chain, and protected transitions. `M2` and `M3` both depend on M1, so every later membership,
fencing, and pursuit claim rests on the core admitted here. Prerequisites are M0 plus the contracts this stage registers.

## In scope

- `M1.1` define and register the package/release manifest, genesis, constitution, amendment,
  ordering/admission/finality, oracle/evidence, lifecycle, and optional enrollment contracts.
- `M1.2` compile immutable package content into a proposed genesis root without creating live state.
- `M1.3` admit genesis through the owning authority, daemon, and Agentgres boundaries.
- `M1.4` freeze active component/profile refs and sequence-zero operation, receipt, transition, and state roots:
  byte-verified M1.3 source material, caller-supplied expected-root comparison, server-side derivation under the
  distinct `scope:autonomous_system.genesis_materialize`, durable preparation before wallet consumption, convergence
  through four no-clobber Agentgres domains.
- `M1.5` implement distinct protected amendment, migration/succession, suspension, dissolution, and enrollment
  transitions, phased as: initialize/activate prefix — chain and operation log exactly `0 -> 1 -> 2` under distinct
  `autonomous_system.lifecycle` scopes with sealed intents, fail-closed wallet consumption, locked no-clobber graph
  persistence; (b) generic protected operational transitions — 14 ops over the canonized chain states at sequence
  `>= 3` via proposal/decision contracts with closed authority-effect objects, a lifecycle-state envelope, a
  parameterized transitions route pair over the sealed-intent machinery, and a census epoch; (c) amendment execution —
  an approved amendment executes on the live chain exactly once under the constitution's own declared amendment
  governance, protected clauses unamendable, predecessor retained as superseded evidence, rollback forward-only;
  (d) migration/succession, dissolution with residual disposition, and `local_only` network enrollment, each under its
  named receipt owner.
- `M1.6` prove two genesis operations from one package create distinct stable Systems without shared live state; every
  cross-System substitution of system identity, package root, profile root, projection policy, or live-state reference
  fails or stays visibly separate.
- `M1.7` expose compact preview, advanced declaration, blocker, and post-genesis read projections without permanent
  Systems navigation, including the compact/advanced/restart/two-System product and operator state matrix.
- Pulled product surfaces: Studio package/genesis composition, Governance preview, Packages lifecycle, and provisional
  System detail.

## Out of scope

- Node membership, topology, writer/finality authority, placement, and restore/activation cleanup (M2); generic pursuit
  and the direct non-System path (M3).
- Consensus, replication, multi-node continuity, settlement, and runtime or workload execution.
- Permanent Systems rail navigation or product Systems UX beyond the pulled read projections; marketplace publication
  as a substitute for local admission.

## Exit proof

The stage exit is owned by the `m1-selected-profile-exit-proof` aggregate named in `program/sequence.v1.json`, joining
M1.1–M1.7 and all protected-transition children, including migration/succession, residual disposition, dissolution, and
local-only enrollment. It closes only when:

- malformed, mismatched, stale, or ordinary-authority attempts admit nothing;
- protected fields change only through their declared authority paths;
- genesis, active constitution, profiles, roots, and receipts reconstruct after restart;
- the package remains reusable and contains no live lease, credential, gateway, run, node, or System identity;
- each child retains its own literal — `M1_GENESIS_ADMISSION_EXIT=0`, `M1_SEQUENCE_ZERO_MATERIALIZATION_EXIT=0`,
  `M1_GOVERNED_INITIALIZE_ACTIVATE_EXIT=0`, `M1_5B_GENERIC_PROTECTED_TRANSITIONS_EXIT=0`,
  `M1_5C_AMENDMENT_EXECUTION_EXIT=0`, `M1_PROTECTED_MIGRATION_DISSOLUTION_ENROLLMENT_EXIT=0`,
  `M1_5_PROTECTED_TRANSITIONS_EXIT=0`, `M1_DUAL_GENESIS_AND_READ_PROJECTION_EXIT=0`, and
  `M1_SYSTEM_GENESIS_PRODUCT_JOURNEY_EXIT=0`;
- retained evidence contains exactly one literal `M1_EXIT=0`, and the aggregate manufactures no child status.

## Blocks advancement

- The `M0` dependency edge, unclosed.
- Any child whose literal exit is not retained, or a missing/duplicate child, stale literal, or incompatible selected
  profile in the aggregate manifest.
- Predeclared adversarial branches left unproven: wrong-scope substitution across transition kinds, stale predecessor
  root, replayed bootstrap evidence, protected-path amendment without the required path, transitions against illegal
  predecessor states per the op-by-state legality matrix, enrollment self-inheritance, restart mid-intent divergence,
  forged decision refs, and cross-System substitution.
- `PG-*` gates: these records declare none applicable, and a gate that becomes applicable is mapped in the owning
  record without redefining it. No external gate is declared for this stage.
- The unresolved canon gap below, for the M1.5d dissolution slice.

## Nonclaims

- No multi-node continuity, public consensus, IOI Network assurance, or general correctness.
- No membership/topology (M2), consensus, writer/finality, settlement, or product Systems UX beyond the pulled read
  projections.
- Activation creates no runtime or workload execution; GoalRun is untouched and no swarm object, conductor, peer
  runtime, or new authority plane appears.
- The package remains reusable and contains no live lease, credential, gateway, run, node, or System identity.
- Enrollment records are local evidence, not IOI Network assurance; `degraded` is an observed posture, never an
  operation target.
- Notice and challenge windows are declared governance evidence, not clock-enforced runtime gates, in the
  single-authority M1 profile.
- Amendment execution, migration/succession, dissolution, and enrollment remain named-owner slices until their records
  close.
