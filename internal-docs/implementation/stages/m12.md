# M12 — AIIP Federation Contract

Let separately sovereign Systems negotiate and exchange bounded work without sharing an operational database, administrator, authority plane, or raw private context.

## Why this is next

Canonical build step 12. The `depends_on` edges in [`sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json) are `M11`, `M5`, and `M7`: M11 establishes useful same-System distributed work and the embodied pre-live boundary, M5 establishes the participant, acceptance, and challenge contracts, and M7 establishes the semantic definition and action plane. M12 adds the first crossing of an independently governed System boundary — the point at which shared envelope conventions become AIIP rather than intra-System coordination — and supplies M13 with its prerequisite. It pulls the `cooperation`, `proof-and-evidence`, and `wallet-authority` modules bound in `sequence.v1.json`.

## In scope

- `M12.1` implement policy-bound OutcomeRoom discovery.
- `M12.2` negotiate exact-root CollaborationTerms covering scope, roles, rights, obligations, disclosure, attribution/reward basis, risk, exit, dispute, and settlement.
- `M12.3` prove accept, counteroffer, decline, expiry, and non-retroactive amendment.
- `M12.4` negotiate versioned ontology/action profiles and mapping risk.
- `M12.5` carry signed, sequenced, idempotent, policy-permitted refs and restricted views through AIIP.
- `M12.6` normalize A2A, MCP, directory/schema, HTTP/RPC, and settlement bindings without making them IOI authority or assurance.
- `M12.7` implement hosted and federated admission modes with explicit ordering, merge, conflict, adjudication, and failover behavior.
- `M12.8` preserve independent challenges, acceptance, contribution lineage, dispute state, revocation, and portable participant exit.
- Own `dispute-adjudication-remedy-kernel` for AIIP and cross-rail admission/appeal/remedy binding after the M5 acceptance/challenge and M8 billing/neutrality exits. M13–M14 may aggregate its evidence but cannot settle before its literal exit.
- Require the existing M12 selected-profile aggregate to join the dispute-kernel record and prevent it from emitting its literal exit while that child remains open.
- Channel prerequisite: a bilaterally admitted AIIP channel, signed and sequenced envelope family, exact profile negotiation, and a mandatory root and receipt manifest between distinct Systems.
- Cross-boundary IFC, disclosure, receipt, and settlement-intent binding for every accepted packet.
- Pulled product surfaces: Goal Space invited/open modes, Work / Rooms discovery, Governance collaboration terms, Ontology mappings, Developer Console federation, Provenance.

## Out of scope

- The two-sovereign Internet-of-Intelligence demonstration and its independently operated Systems (M13).
- Network services, optional L1 enrollment, marketplaces, and economics (M14).
- Same-`system_id` replication, failover, RuntimeAssignment, mission allocation, fleet coordination, and work leases, which remain native L0/Embodied Runtime concerns.
- Live embodied promotion and any foreign physical-control path.
- Bond escrow refund/payout execution reserved to `PG-6B.3`, which this stage does not select.
- Self-adjudication, evidence loss, settlement before finality, and remedy without appeal.

## Exit proof

The stage exit gate in [`sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json) is the `aggregate_exit` work item `m12-selected-profile-exit-proof`. For the selected profile — two separately sovereign Systems negotiating exact-root AIIP terms — it may emit its literal exit only when all of the following hold:

- each System remains locally complete when it declines or disconnects;
- neither peer can mutate the other's truth, widen its authority, or send a direct foreign physical-control stream;
- external participants retain permitted portable state without continued access to an IOI-hosted room;
- protocol completion does not imply verification, acceptance, settlement, or correctness;
- every unconditional child in the aggregate binding is closed, including `dispute-adjudication-remedy-kernel`, whose own literal exit must precede any aggregate emission;
- retained evidence contains exactly one literal `M12_EXIT=0`, bound to exact artifact bytes.

Each child carries its own literal exit contract — `M12_AIIP_CHANNEL_ENVELOPE_PROFILE_EXIT=0`, `M12_TERMS_DISCOVERY_SEMANTIC_NEGOTIATION_EXIT=0`, `M12_FEDERATED_ADMISSION_PORTABLE_EXIT_AND_BINDINGS_EXIT=0`, `M12_IFC_DISCLOSURE_SETTLEMENT_BINDING_EXIT=0`, `DISPUTE_ADJUDICATION_REMEDY_EXIT=0`, `M12_FEDERATION_PRODUCT_JOURNEY_EXIT=0` — none of which the aggregate may substitute for.

## Blocks advancement

- Any unmet `depends_on` edge: verified M11 plus the M5 participant and M7 semantic contracts.
- The M5 acceptance/challenge exit and the M8 billing/neutrality exits, which the dispute kernel binds after.
- An open `dispute-adjudication-remedy-kernel` child, which holds the aggregate literal exit.
- Unevidenced applicable gates: `PG-2.5`, `PG-6B.1`, `PG-6B.2`, `PG-6B.4`, `PG-6B.5`.
- A missing or duplicate child, an unresolved dispute, a stale literal, or a task/process exit code offered in place of the literal.
- Any unresolved canon gap below, which must be settled in its named canon owner or an accepted ADR rather than inside a work item.

## Nonclaims

- Positive economic value or an Internet-of-Intelligence proof.
- No M13 two-sovereign proof, surplus, correctness, acceptance, or settlement claim.
- AIIP completion establishes no verification, acceptance, settlement, correctness, positive-surplus, marketplace, or network-effect claim.
- Transport, socket, or node identity does not establish sovereignty.
- Federated admission creates no shared runtime, database, administrator, or authority.
- External protocol interoperability is not AIIP authority or verification.
- No raw private context, shared database, settlement, or positive surplus.
- No foreign physical-control stream.
