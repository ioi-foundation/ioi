# M0 — Program Control And Claim Lock

Freeze what may be claimed, prove the program-control model, and lock the claim ladder before any product stage advances.

## Why this is next

M0 carries `depends_on: []` in [`sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json): it is the graph root, inherits no predecessor state, and must establish its own ground. It fixes the owner map, the route/effect/method censuses, the selected profile, the frozen thresholds, and the disposition of every prior plan, so a downstream stage cannot silently import an unowned object or an unbounded claim. M1 declares `depends_on: ["M0"]` and every later stage reaches M0 transitively; a bounded-System claim is meaningful only once the program can state what it is not claiming.

## In scope

- `M0.1` refresh the source-of-truth owner map and accepted ADR list.
- `M0.2` snapshot the canonical delta and relevant implementation-matrix rows.
- `M0.3` inventory every externally reachable route in one hashed mount manifest, classify it as authenticated, explicitly public, or internal-only, name the final invoker for every consequential path, and prove that any MCP projection re-enters the same authoritative admission and invocation path.
- `M0.4` freeze the selected first deployment profile and nonclaims.
- `M0.5` maintain the source-plan disposition ledger; its durable form is the machine-readable ledger and duplicate-voice verifier owned by `m0-source-disposition-and-single-sequencer-verifier`, not prose inside a guide.
- `M0.6` map all 58 `PG-*` gates to the selected profile without redefining them.
- `M0.7` create the program evidence index, release ladder, and blocker ledger.
- `M0.8` record current product, reliability, cost, and comprehension baselines.
- `M0.9` freeze the complete `RuntimeKernelService` trust-boundary audit. Every public facade method appears exactly once with its canonical owner, authority-crossing posture, truth/effect/receipt relevance, proposed disposition, and required negative proof. Method or line count alone is not an extraction criterion; the audit verifier fails on missing, duplicate, renamed, or newly added unaudited methods.
- The `ioi.program.work_item.v1` record contract, its owner/contract/private-artifact reference lint, and the migration/waiver report.
- The canon-owner-to-stage/record coverage generator with orphan rejection: zero owners, multiple owners, stale digest, archive-as-owner, public queue.
- The machine-checked retained-log contract for literal proof-bar exit values, with predeclared missing, duplicate, conflicting, malformed, stale, truncated, non-success, and green-process/no-literal fixtures that each fail closed.
- The unsigned M0 review anchor (`ioi.m0.review_epoch_anchor.v3`): sequences 1–6 retained verbatim as immutable legacy claims; new epochs declare `authorship_binding: self_declared_unsigned`.
- Census re-entry: every new externally reachable route re-enters the M0.3 census and the unsigned workflow hash-chain review.

## Out of scope

- Instantiating a bounded System, genesis, or constitutional lifecycle — M1.
- Any macro runtime cut migrating a route family's admission, authority, receipt, or projection ownership — M1 and later, under `R-RUNTIME`.
- Redefining any `PG-*` gate; M0.6 maps applicability only, and the `mechanism-gates` module owns the definitions.
- Closing any claim-ladder level; M0's `closes_claim_levels` is empty.
- Deleting or rewriting superseded sources; dispositions and tombstones only.
- Granting product authority from workflow evidence, review anchors, or audit ledgers — the wallet-network authority model owns that.

## Exit proof

The M0 exit is owned by the single aggregate record `m0-program-control-selected-profile-exit-proof`, named as M0's `aggregate_exit` in [`sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json). It joins M0.1–M0.9, all existing and approved M0 records, selected PG applicability, the exact runtime-trust/route/final-invoker censuses, the frozen profile, baselines and nonclaims, the source dispositions, and the single-sequencer check.

For that record to close, all of the following must hold:

- every selected object has an owner;
- every selected effect has a final invoker;
- the live `RuntimeKernelService` public-method inventory matches the audit exactly, and no proposed extraction is mistaken for completed trust-boundary proof;
- every old plan has an explicit disposition and work-item mapping;
- the selected profile, thresholds, nonclaims, and rollback rules are frozen;
- no ignored guide still functions as a competing sequencer;
- retained evidence contains exactly one literal `M0_EXIT=0`, and each of the ten child records contributes its own retained literal — a task or process exit code, UI state, plan, or the aggregate itself cannot substitute.

## Blocks advancement

- Any of the ten child records failing to close; the aggregate has no independent path.
- A missing or duplicate M0 obligation; a stale owner, route, or method census; or a second document exercising sequencer voice.
- An externally reachable route absent from the hashed mount manifest, or a consequential path whose final invoker cannot be named and guarded.
- A `RuntimeKernelService` method missing, duplicated, renamed, or newly added without an audit row.
- `PG-*` applicability for the selected profile left unmapped, or a gate whose definition is altered rather than referenced.
- Any unresolved entry under "Canon gaps"; M0 may not resolve canon doctrine itself.
- No external gates are declared for M0 in the sequencer or in any M0 record, so none can be cited as a reason to advance or to wait.

## Nonclaims

- M0 does not close any architecture or production capability. `M0_EXIT=0` closes no architecture or production capability, and closes no claim-ladder level.
- Program control is workflow evidence and grants no product authority.
- The docs-and-orchestration records close no work item, stage, runtime capability, application, or public claim. Creating a record does not implement behavior, activate work, close a literal exit, or change a stage.
- Unsigned workflow hash chains grant no product authority. The review anchor is development-workflow integrity evidence only: it establishes no authorship, no accepted-head currentness, and no rollback resistance between coherent snapshots. Wallet grants, sealed intents, and receipts remain the product authority model.
- The unsigned-anchor cut closes no M0 stage semantics; the M0 exit claim is unchanged in scope. Any semantic scope beyond that cut's pre-migration objective, required-work labels, owners, and contract families requires a separate proposed successor record.
- A proposed extraction is not completed trust-boundary proof.
