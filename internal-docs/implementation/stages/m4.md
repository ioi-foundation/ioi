# M4 — OutcomeRoom As The Flagship Bounded DAS

Compile the reusable OutcomeRoom package through genesis into a real bounded-System instance whose
shared graph is cryptographically continuous.

## Why this is next

`sequence.v1.json` places M4 at canonical build step 4 with one `depends_on` edge to M3, whose direct pursuit-and-result seam supplies the generic `WorkResult` and `OutcomeDelta` a room child may carry; M1 and M2 are reached transitively and supply the genesis, constitution, profile, deployment, and Agentgres truth that room genesis compiles against. M4 adds the first reusable-package-to-live-System composition and proves that shared-graph mutations use ordinary Agentgres expected-head preconditions plus the enclosing System's predecessor continuity. It adds no room-owned CAS or admission spine. M4 verification closes M4 only. P0 requires the joint M3, M4, and M5 closure and is not claimed here.

## In scope

- `M4.1` bind package/release, room genesis, stable `system_id`, constitution, deployment, admission mode, oracle/evidence, lifecycle, and root set.
- `M4.2` require `SystemScopedObjectBinding` on every room-scoped typed payload and prove the enclosing Agentgres operation carries the exact expected head(s), resolved authority/policy evidence, receipt refs, and System predecessor condition.
- `M4.3` prove every shared-state transition carries its compare-and-swap condition as the enclosing Agentgres operation's expected head(s), preserves the bounded System predecessor chain, and fails closed on conflict; no room object owns a parallel revision, decision, commitment, root, or receipt sequence.
- `M4.4` bind reciprocal room/GoalRun membership without making OutcomeRoom a runtime or GoalRun a global collaboration graph.
- `M4.5` implement hosted admission honestly as one declared domain rather than a shared-database shortcut.
- `M4.6` expose room lifecycle, graph head, blockers, authority, costs, and evidence from reconstructable truth.
- Owner scopes `PROGRAM`, `ROOMS`, `SYSTEM`, `AGENTGRES`, `SURFACES`, plus the private child/evidence join artifact `M4SelectedProfileEvidence`.
- Freeze the crossed contract families as typed room-scoped shapes associated through `SystemScopedObjectBinding`: `OutcomeRoom`, `CollaborativeWorkGraph`, `WorkFrontierItem`, `WorkClaimLease`, `Attempt`, `Finding`, `VerifierChallenge`, `WorkResult`, `OutcomeDelta`, `ParticipantStateBundle`, `ReceiptEnvelope`. The enclosing bounded-System operation admits each mutation through Agentgres. The complete freeze is derived mechanically from the `contract_families` arrays of both active M4 records and retained in `program/m4-contract-freeze.v1.json`; no hand-curated list controls the check. M4 exercises only its minimum room `WorkResult`/`OutcomeDelta`; the participant/frontier/claim/attempt/finding/challenge lifecycle families remain honest-empty M5 nonclaims.
- Split truth and replay from the *minimum* Goal Space / Work / Governance / Provenance proof projections; pulled product surfaces are ioi.ai Goal Space, Work / Rooms, Systems context, Governance, Provenance.
- Rebind the restored product shell's existing composer in place for the selected M4 proof journey. Ordinary submit/Enter remains Session-only. A separately labelled activation affordance drafts, reviews, and submits `GoalRunActivationEnvelope` with `source_kind: ioi_goal_draft`; only daemon admission may create GoalRun truth. The existing shell and composer remain the executable integration seed under ADR 0028; QM is the starting reference UX for the ioi.ai web surface. This is an implementation disposition, not canon or a parity claim, and it authorizes no `apps/ioi-ai/`, parallel React shell, replacement Home, or app-by-app M6 cutover. The minimum Goal Space projection consumes daemon-owned GoalRun/OutcomeRoom state and owns no graph, admission, credential, execution, receipt, or replay truth.
- Positive, adversarial, stale-state, substitution, fault, recovery, product/operator-state, and retained-evidence planning for the bounded claim under the selected profile: one hosted OutcomeRoom compiled as a bounded System.
- Map PG-3.5 (room discussion, artifact-byte resolution, durable label lookup/persistence without a second truth owner) as `required_now` here.

## Work items

### Stage entry shape (binding within M4, not a second sequencer)

M4 starts by load-testing the M3 seam through the cheapest honest first-party
product path; it does not begin by assuming that every goal needs a room.

1. **Explicit Goal Space activation lane.** Preserve the restored native
   composer and its Session-default submit path. Add one separate, explicit
   activation review act that carries `ioi_goal_draft` through typed source,
   principal, profile, authority, review, daemon admission, activation receipts,
   restart, and reconstruction of the same roomless, resultless GoalRun head. The
   verified M3 `direct_non_system` seam is a consumed fixture and regression
   anchor, not an ioi.ai activation substitute and not work to redo. This lane
   materializes no OutcomeRoom, `WorkResult`, or `OutcomeDelta` and closes no M4
   claim by itself.
2. **Truth and proof projection.** Bind the resultless direct regression and then room state to
   reconstructable Goal Space, Work, Governance, Provenance, discussion, and
   receipt projections. A client write, stale head, missing label, substituted
   artifact, or unavailable projection must remain visibly unavailable and
   cannot be repaired in client state.
3. **OutcomeRoom increment.** Only after the direct lane and proof projection
   are standing, compile the reusable room package through genesis and add the
   hosted admission, reciprocal GoalRun membership, minimum room `WorkResult` /
   `OutcomeDelta` transitions, and honest-empty M5 lifecycle boundary required
   by the aggregate exit.

This ordering is internal to the two M4 records below. It does not alter
`sequence.v1.json`, create a new stage status, weaken the aggregate exit, or
move the participant/frontier/result lifecycle proof owned by M5 into M4.

## Exit proof

Bound to the `aggregate_exit` work item `m4-outcome-room-system-spine` and its literal `M4_OUTCOME_ROOM_SYSTEM_SPINE_EXIT=0`, retained under `internal-docs/implementation/evidence/M4/`. All of the following must hold:

- every room transition refuses stale heads, unbound actors, wrong System, missing roots, changed bodies, and direct client writes;
- package-to-genesis and room-child lineage verifies from exported evidence;
- the hosted aggregate cannot bypass the bounded-System constitution or the Agentgres admission path;
- the child `m4-room-graph-truth-and-product-projection` closes with its own literal `M4_ROOM_GRAPH_TRUTH_PRODUCT_EXIT=0`, refusing direct UI graph writes, wrong-System children, stale verdicts, and export leakage;
- the frozen thresholds hold, including `m4.direct_client_shared_graph_writes = 0` and exactly one retained literal exit line per record.

These clauses are unchanged by ADR 0031. They contain no GoalRun execution,
thread, fork, managed-session, launch-recipe, or harness-binding claim. Passing
them must not be presented as closure of the separately recorded orchestration
seam.

## Blocks advancement

- The `depends_on: [M3]` edge, and the child record's dependency on `m3-direct-path-and-exit-proof`.
- The unconditional child `m4-room-graph-truth-and-product-projection`: the aggregate cannot verify on an umbrella-only claim, a missing room-truth/product child, or a stale literal.
- PG-3.5, mapped `required_now` with closure stage M4, blocks only while its exact child literal is absent or invalid; it closes through retained evidence and the typed child transition, never through prose or a development run.
- Empty or `literal_valid: false` evidence bindings in the aggregate verification binding, which is a closure precondition and promotes nothing on its own.
- The reopened R-CONTRACT ruling below: M4 cannot close until the typed successor families and `SystemScopedObjectBinding` are registry-resolved and the runtime no longer depends on `RoomAdmittedObjectBase`.
- Any attempt to label the composer's Session/transcript path as an ioi.ai GoalRun. `GoalRunActivationEnvelope`, its source/authority/profile admission, durable store, receipt, idempotency refusal, and runnable conformance tier must be implemented before the product projection can advance.
- Any room-family schema that resolves in the registry but does not enforce the typed room binding, System/profile/constitution, conditional, and cross-field invariants required by its canonical owner. Registry presence alone is not contract closure.

## M4 readiness rulings after ADR 0030 (2026-07-31)

1. **REOPENED — room contract closure.** The 2026-07-30 ruling closed R-CONTRACT partly by registering `RoomAdmittedObjectBase` v2. ADR 0030 removes that admission base, so the historical child verification is qualified by an open corrective-successor hold and cannot count as current closure. The registry now owns `SystemScopedObjectBinding` plus typed v3 successor shapes for the affected room families. R-CONTRACT closes again only when the runtime and successor proof consume those contracts through ordinary Agentgres/System admission and reject the retired parallel-spine fields.
2. **Reciprocal room/GoalRun membership has one authority rule.** `docs/architecture/foundations/objects/collaborative-pursuit.md` makes the room's `member_goal_run_refs` the aggregate membership authority, keeps `GoalRun.outcome_room_ref` authoritative for that GoalRun's single backlink, and requires an atomic exact-head attach/detach receipt. A mismatch is typed unavailable truth, never a client-side repair.
3. **Room admission refusals have a conformance owner, but successor execution is open.** `docs/conformance/hypervisor-core/outcome-room-admission.md`, indexed by `docs/conformance/README.md`, owns positive admission plus wrong-System, stale-head, direct-write, parallel-spine, label, lineage, challenge, crash/replay, and export refusal cases. The legacy aggregate cannot adjudicate the successor contract merely by staying green.
4. **Room discussion projections have a durable owner and schema.** `docs/architecture/foundations/objects/collaborative-pursuit.md` and `docs/architecture/components/agentgres/projection-system-reference.md` own `OutcomeRoomDiscussionProjectionEnvelope`; the registry owns its schema and fixtures. It binds the exact canonical Agentgres/System transition and source receipts, information-flow labels, referenced durable objects, and redactions, and is explicitly derived and non-client-writable.
5. **Predecessor room writes are retired, not an M4 compatibility lane.** `docs/architecture/components/daemon-runtime/api.md` owns the canonical route family as bounded-System v2 only. Historical v1 records remain source-disposition fixtures; non-v2 create/lifecycle/membership writes and occupied-slot reads refuse typed, list/overview omit them, and M4 introduces no public compatibility route. M5 participant/frontier/attempt/finding/challenge lifecycle verifiers are therefore not pulled forward as M4 focused checks.
6. **Room admission consumes, and never silently creates, an active bounded System.** `docs/architecture/components/daemon-runtime/api.md` owns `POST /outcome-rooms` as the room-admission leg of the package-to-genesis composition. The route requires exact already-admitted OutcomeRoom package/release, genesis, constitution, profile, origin, and activation coordinates from the autonomous-System owner paths. A guided product workflow may sequence those explicit acts, but room admission grants no wallet authority and cannot mint or infer System truth.
