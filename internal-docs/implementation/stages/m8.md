# M8 — Enterprise Learning And Bounded Improvement

Make institutional learning owned, portable, provider-neutral, and improvable without letting search judge or promote itself.

## Why this is next

`sequence.v1.json` places M8 at canonical build step 8 with one `depends_on` edge to M7, whose aggregate `m7-semantic-definition-action-plane` supplies the local ontology and action semantics that every M8 cut names as a dependency. M7 establishes what an institution's definitions and actions mean; M8 establishes who owns what those actions teach, which provider may ever see it, and how a bounded Campaign may propose an improvement without judging or promoting itself. Two cuts additionally reach back to `m5-attribution-acceptance-and-challenge-boundary` for local worker supply and routing neutrality. Nothing in M8 reaches the M12 dispute kernel, which is what keeps M8 and M12 acyclic.

## In scope

- `M8.1` compile source rights, consent, policy-bound views, custody posture, provider/customer route rights, evidence eligibility, retention/export, derivative lineage, jurisdiction, and revocation into one admitted `InstitutionalLearningBoundaryProfile`.
- `M8.2` snapshot the effective boundary into Systems, Sessions, GoalRuns, model invocations, transformations, evaluations, and Foundry jobs.
- `M8.3` block forbidden provider/cross-tenant egress before network or model invocation and issue `LearningEgressReceipt` for permitted crossings.
- `M8.4` export/import only rights-eligible institutional intelligence with transitive lineage and explicit exclusions.
- `M8.5` hard-disable one provider and meet frozen model-independence floors through an eligible replacement route using institution-controlled state.
- `M8.6` prove source revocation triggers quarantine, reevaluation, recall, or clean rebuild according to policy.
- `M8.7` admit immutable `ImprovementGovernanceProfile`, revisioned Agenda, finite Campaign, exact target base, frozen `EvaluationEpoch`, exposure ledger, order cutoff, and evidence claim.
- `M8.8` identify Search, Judgment, and Authority separately, retain negative evidence, enforce finite budgets, reproduce selection against the unchanged epoch, and emit only a target-owner `UpgradeProposal`.
- `M8.9` preserve the direct one-shot proposal path for work that does not need a Campaign.
- Own `foundry-evaluations-production-loop`, `worker-training-and-local-marketplace-supply`, `managed-billing-work-credits-and-supplier-reconciliation`, and `marketplace-neutral-routing-contribution-accounting` as reusable local/managed mechanisms. These record IDs contain no federation or connected-service work.
- Own the Evaluations, Improvement, and Foundry operational-journey records.
- Require the M8 aggregate to join all four distinct literal exits. Local worker supply depends on the M5 acceptance/challenge boundary and billing; neutrality depends on the local-worker and billing exits.
- Pull the product surfaces Governance Enterprise Learning Boundary, Foundry, Evaluations, Improvement, Models/routes, Provenance, and Packages, each with policy-filtered empty, denied, unavailable, degraded, recovery, and completed states.

## Out of scope

- The M12 dispute-adjudication and remedy kernel; M8 must take no dependency on it.
- Federation, connected services, and any cross-sovereign learning exchange.
- Silent provider substitution, learning-right inference, missing application or conditional child, mutable floors or stop rules, and campaign status asserted from prose.
- Preferential or opaque allocation, attribution theft, settlement before local challenge finality, non-portable evidence, double charge, unmetered effect, stale quote, unreconciled supplier, and native-asset inference.
- Training/evaluation leakage, mutable epochs, non-independent judgment, and promotion without a bundle.
- Claim-level publication: `sequence.v1.json` records no claim level closed at M8.

## Exit proof

The stage exit is the aggregate work item `m8-model-supply-route-substitution-and-selected-exit` named in `sequence.v1.json`, whose retained evidence must contain exactly one literal `M8_EXIT=0`. The aggregate must join the Evaluations, Improvement, and Foundry literal exits, and must join all four distinct literal exits of the reusable mechanism records (`FOUNDRY_EVALUATIONS_PRODUCTION_EXIT=0`, `LOCAL_WORKER_MARKETPLACE_SUPPLY_EXIT=0`, `MANAGED_BILLING_RECONCILIATION_EXIT=0`, `MARKETPLACE_NEUTRALITY_ACCOUNTING_EXIT=0`). Shell presence, suite declaration, proposal cards, run plans, a task or process exit code, UI state, or the aggregate itself are not execution proof.

For the stage to be declared complete, all of the following must hold and be evidenced:

- provider removal does not remove institutional ontology, memory, evals, corrections, policies, workflow state, or eligible derived capability;
- prohibited canaries reach no forbidden destination;
- the seller and other tenants cannot retrieve protected learning material;
- the Campaign cannot mutate its selecting evaluator, reset limits by branching, fabricate evidence, promote itself, or mutate production;
- all promotion, activation, rollback, recall, and kill decisions cross the target owner's ordinary governance path.

## Blocks advancement

- An unmet M7 dependency: every M8 cut except the aggregate depends on `m7-semantic-definition-action-plane`; the stage also presupposes selected provider, data, Foundry, evaluation, and rights contracts.
- An unmet `m5-attribution-acceptance-and-challenge-boundary`, which gates `worker-training-and-local-marketplace-supply` and `marketplace-neutral-routing-contribution-accounting`.
- Any unmet internal edge: neutrality depends on the local-worker and billing exits; the improvement journey depends on the evaluations journey and the order-zero cut; the Foundry journey and the Foundry/evaluations loop depend on the custody cut.
- Unsatisfied `PG-3.6` (learning-boundary conformance adapter, contract grade then deployment grade), `PG-0.1` and `PG-0.2` (full `ImprovementCampaign` vertical; route-level anti-decomposition, waiver-freshness, and application-chain receipt proof), and `PG-6A.1`, `PG-6A.4`, `PG-7.6` (supplier reconciliation, durable billing ledger with offline audit export, post-outage accounting convergence).
- Any unresolved canon gap below, and any M8 contract family still lacking a registered schema with generated projections.

## Nonclaims

- No hidden provider non-learning, weight-level forgetting, recursive improvement, ignition, or general optimization.
- No equivalent-quality claim from a model swap; the model-independence result is bounded to the declared benchmark and threshold and claims nothing universal.
- Order zero only: no recursive-seat, recursive-self-improvement, or stage-closure claim; Campaign coordination grants no wallet authority; workflow evidence does not authorize learning or close a stage.
- No neutrality, federation, or connected-service claim arises from the local worker-supply record; no connected-service or native-asset inference arises from billing.
- Creating or updating a record here implements no behavior, activates no work, closes no literal exit, and changes no stage.
