# M5 — Participants, Local Agents, And Shared Frontier

> **HOLD — ADR 0030 (2026-07-31).** M5 must not start while M4's reopened
> room-contract closure is outstanding. Its participant, frontier, claim,
> attempt, finding, challenge, result, and export mutations must target the
> `RoomScopedObjectBinding` successor shapes and ordinary Agentgres expected-head
> plus bounded-System predecessor admission. No M5 work item may build on or
> reintroduce `RoomAdmittedObjectBase` or a room-owned decision, sequence,
> transition, receipt, state-root, receipt-root, or CAS plane.

Let humans and heterogeneous agents contribute useful work to a room without pairing, prompts, rank, or runtime access ever becoming ambient authority.

## Why this is next

`sequence.v1.json` places M5 at canonical build step 5 with `depends_on: ["M4"]`, and every M5 implementation cut names `m4-outcome-room-system-spine` as a dependency edge. M4 must establish the OutcomeRoom composition — the room System, its generic result/delta seam, and its canonical Agentgres/System receipt and root lineage — for actors the host already owns. M5 adds the non-owner contributor to that composition: a paired local agent, an admitted guest participant, a pull-based shared frontier, and an exit path that returns permitted state without host-database dependence. The stage's prerequisites also include the selected identity/authority rail, which is why `wallet-authority` is pulled here.

## In scope

- `M5.1` implement `LocalAgentPairingSession` create, inspect, claim, complete, cancel, expire, and revoke with challenge, key, origin, target, attempt, and single-use binding;
- `M5.2` admit `RoomParticipationRequest` and issue bounded `RoomParticipantLease` only after an explicit decision;
- `M5.3` admit resource and capability offers bound to real inventory and eligibility evidence;
- `M5.4` implement pull-based `WorkFrontierItem` and `WorkClaimLease` transitions with TTL, heartbeat, backpressure, renewal, release, and revocation;
- `M5.5` bind each `Attempt` to one claim and one bounded GoalRun;
- `M5.6` admit positive, negative, exploit, invalid, and inconclusive `Finding` records with evidence;
- `M5.7` admit `VerifierChallenge`, resolution, and acceptance-blocking rules;
- `M5.8` admit room-scoped WorkResult and OutcomeDelta as typed Agentgres operations through the exact participant/lease binding, expected head(s), resolved authority/policy evidence, and enclosing-System predecessor/receipt/root chain;
- `M5.9` export a policy-filtered `ParticipantStateBundle` and release all claims on exit or revocation;
- pairing intent, session, and expiry bound to identity, with post-admission capability and gateway scope narrowed rather than left broad (`IDENTITY`, `CONNECTORS`, `SECURITY`);
- the acceptance boundary across `ROOMS`, `ECON`, `WALLET`, and `DAEMON`: attribution that is not acceptance, a challenged result that cannot settle, stale lease and reassignment handling, and retained negative evidence that is not lost;
- authenticated time bound into lease TTL, heartbeat, expiry, and forged-time denial;
- the two-independent-client conformance path and an export bundle verifiable without trusting the host database;
- the eight declared product/operator states on every M5 surface, including honest empty, denied/revoked, unavailable/degraded, stale/conflict, and recovery;
- a non-authoritative P0 readiness manifest joining selected M3–M5 literal exits, a passing direct-path regression, and one controlled owner-boundary fixture;
- the aggregate selected-profile adversarial proof over pairing, admission, frontier, result lineage, revocation, and retained negative evidence.

Selected profile for the stage's records: hosted room participation with one proposal-only local agent, bounded leases, and portable filtered exit.

## Out of scope

- public marketplace publication, listing, routing eligibility, reputation, and payout — owned by the marketplace and economics owners at a later horizon;
- federation, AIIP channel work, and cross-domain admission — M12;
- claim-bearing cohort qualification and public claim estate — M9;
- honest product workspaces and application-catalog compilation over M1–M5 read models — M6;
- acceptance verdicts, settlement, and dispute adjudication as owner concerns beyond the boundary this stage must not cross;
- production operation, multi-node topology, and matched-performance claims;
- independent-sovereignty claims of any kind arising from the portable exit bundle.

## Exit proof

The stage exit is `m5-selected-profile-exit-proof`. It may be declared only when all of the following hold for the selected profile and are bound to retained evidence:

- pairing grants no membership, context, budget, tools, reputation, payout, or authority;
- a guest can propose but cannot self-admit results or effects;
- replay, origin mismatch, wrong target, exceeded attempts, expired lease, and revoked participant all fail closed;
- revocation releases claims and preserves attribution and evidence;
- at least two independently implemented clients complete pairing, participation, one claim, contribution, and exit;
- the child manifest is closed with no missing or duplicate child, no challenged-as-accepted result, no unsafe portable exit, and no stale literal or task-code substitution;
- retained evidence contains exactly one literal `M5_SELECTED_PROFILE_EXIT_PROOF_EXIT=0`.

Each child carries its own literal, none of which the aggregate may substitute for: `M5_LOCAL_AGENT_PAIRING_EXIT=0`, `M5_PAIRING_IDENTITY_GATEWAY_SCOPE_EXIT=0`, `M5_PARTICIPANT_FRONTIER_RESULT_CLOSEOUT_EXIT=0`, `M5_PORTABLE_EXIT_INDEPENDENT_CLIENTS_EXIT=0`, `M5_ATTRIBUTION_ACCEPTANCE_CHALLENGE_EXIT=0`. The private verifier carries `M5_P0_READINESS_VERIFIER_EXIT=0` and is not part of the stage exit gate.

## Blocks advancement

- the restated M4 successor gate not being verified and its open corrective-successor hold not being discharged, since every M5 cut depends on current rather than historical M4 closure;
- the selected identity/authority rail not being in place, per the stage prerequisite;
- unmet intra-stage edges: `m5-pairing-identity-and-gateway-scope` after `m5-local-agent-pairing`; `m5-portable-exit-independent-clients` and `m5-attribution-acceptance-and-challenge-boundary` after `m5-participant-frontier-result-closeout`;
- for `m5-p0-readiness-verifier` only, `m3-direct-path-and-exit-proof` in addition to the M4 and M5 aggregate edges;
- any `external_gates` entry declared on an M5 record;
- any `PG-*` gate that [`modules/mechanism-gates.md`](../modules/mechanism-gates.md) maps onto a mechanism this stage exercises, mapped on the owning record without changing its definition;
- unresolved canon gaps below, which must be closed by their named canon owner rather than inside a work item.

## Nonclaims

- no public marketplace publication, federation, correctness, or payout;
- pre-AIIP and proposal-only: no publication, reputation, payout, or federation from pairing;
- pairing is not participation and not authority, and closes no stage;
- hosted mode only; acceptance, verdict, settlement, federation, and stage closure remain separate owner concerns;
- portability covers permitted state only; it is not federation, payout, or an independent-sovereignty claim;
- no M9 claim-bearing cohort claim; P0 remains a walking skeleton with no production, multi-node, federation, or matched-performance claim;
- no M5 status transition occurs without committed literal exit evidence.

## Canon gaps

- **Contract registration for the M5 object family.** Nineteen of the twenty-two contract families declared by M5 records resolve only through `reviewed_owner_locator` with empty `contract_ids` and `schema_versions` — including `LocalAgentPairingSessionEnvelope`, `RoomParticipantLeaseEnvelope`, `WorkFrontierItemEnvelope`, `WorkClaimLeaseEnvelope`, `AttemptEnvelope`, `FindingEnvelope`, `VerifierChallengeEnvelope`, `WorkResultEnvelope`/`OutcomeDeltaEnvelope`, and `ParticipantStateBundleEnvelope`. `R-CONTRACT` and the completion bundle require a registered schema and a generated Rust/TypeScript form. Canon owners to resolve: [`foundations/common-objects-and-envelopes.md`](../../../docs/architecture/foundations/common-objects-and-envelopes.md) and [`_meta/schemas/architecture-contract-registry.v1.json`](../../../docs/architecture/_meta/schemas/architecture-contract-registry.v1.json).
- **Definition of an independently implemented client.** The exit requires two independently implemented clients, but no canon or conformance owner defines what independence requires — separate transport, separate codegen, separate authoring party, or merely a separate binary. Canon owners to resolve: ADR [`0002`](../../../docs/decisions/0002-execution-authority-and-client-boundaries.md) and [`components/hypervisor/core-clients-surfaces.md`](../../../docs/architecture/components/hypervisor/core-clients-surfaces.md).
- **Authenticated time source.** Lease TTL, heartbeat, expiry, and forged-time denial all turn on authenticated time, but no foundations or daemon-runtime owner defines the trusted time source, its trust boundary, skew tolerance, or the denial semantics on skew. Canon owners to resolve: [`foundations/invariants.md`](../../../docs/architecture/foundations/invariants.md) and [`components/daemon-runtime/platform-operability.md`](../../../docs/architecture/components/daemon-runtime/platform-operability.md).
- **Frontier backpressure and fairness vocabulary.** Canon names queue backpressure and fair resource allocation as required room controls and carries a `fairness_and_backpressure_policy_refs` field, but does not define the admissible policy vocabulary or what a participant observes when backpressure denies a claim. Canon owners to resolve: [`domains/ioi-ai/collaborative-outcome-pattern.md`](../../../docs/architecture/domains/ioi-ai/collaborative-outcome-pattern.md) and [`components/daemon-runtime/events-receipts-delivery-bundles.md`](../../../docs/architecture/components/daemon-runtime/events-receipts-delivery-bundles.md).
