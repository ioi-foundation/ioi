# Enterprise-Owned Learning And Provider Exit

## What this module owns

Proving in one chain — not as separate plane passes — that governed institutional material improves institution-controlled
capability, stays unavailable to prohibited providers, sellers, and tenants, survives removal of an incumbent cognition
provider, and carries rights changes through its derivatives. Method only: it orders no work, carries no status, is never a
sequencer.

## Pulled by

`M8`, per `modules[].applies_to_stages` for `learning-boundary` in [`program/sequence.v1.json`](../program/sequence.v1.json).
The retiring work packet framed these obligations over an M7–M9 window plus an enterprise release overlay after M9; that
framing is retained as applicability, never as a binding — only the sequencer binds a stage to a module.

## Retained obligations

Composed owners: Institutional Learning Boundary, model router, Data, Ontology, Foundry, Evaluations, Agentgres, Private
Workspace, Governance, Provenance. The boundary composes them, never absorbs them, and adds no authority plane, truth store,
runtime, or rights generator. The required proof, retained exactly:

```text
private correction and eval admitted under one boundary revision
  -> forbidden route blocked before egress
  -> eligible improvement with transitive lineage
  -> incumbent provider hard-disabled
  -> replacement route meets frozen floors using institution-owned state
  -> seller and cross-tenant access denied
  -> eligible capability exported and imported into a clean deployment
  -> one source revoked
  -> impact quarantined, reevaluated, recalled, or rebuilt as policy declares
```

- **One boundary revision.** Both artifacts enter under one compiled effective profile — the most-restrictive deterministic
  intersection of applicable parents — whose policy hash and input revisions appear in a typed compilation receipt. Session,
  run, invocation, transformation, and Foundry-job snapshots stay immutable; no organization or project default widens a
  sovereign pin.
- **Blocked before egress.** Denial precedes DNS, connection, request body, provider invocation, retry, fallback, and
  telemetry. The `LearningEgressReceipt` carries decision, policy hash, recipient/route bindings, material classes and
  commitment, purpose, and gateway or network evidence, never plaintext; fallback may not substitute an incompatible
  provider-trust route, and blocking after transmission is not blocking.
- **Eligible improvement with transitive lineage.** Missing, expired, disputed, or conflicting source rights permit no
  training, distillation, cross-tenant learning, publication, or export; inference or evaluation permission never implies
  training permission. Each admitted input binds the boundary snapshot, rights claims, retention, use, and eligibility
  decision; ineligible copies never reach dataset materialization; every derivative resolves to its sources; the candidate
  stays inert until ordinary promotion, runtime, and authority gates pass.
- **Incumbent provider hard-disabled.** Disablement covers provider credentials, provider-native thread and hidden memory
  state, provider-only tools, and fallbacks — not a preference change — and no incumbent route appears in later invocation or
  network receipts.
- **Frozen floors on institution-owned state.** Benchmark, suite, and minimum thresholds freeze before observation and never
  change retroactively; only institution-controlled, rights-eligible state is restored through the same Hypervisor contracts,
  and the report publishes score delta, every unsupported or nonportable dependency, and the threshold verdict.
- **Seller and cross-tenant denial.** Seller, provider, marketplace, network, and cross-customer learning are default-deny for
  protected material; every unconsented request for the correction, evaluation, memory projection, dataset, candidate,
  aggregate contribution, or canary result is denied and receipted, and package authorship, managed hosting, account
  administration, anonymization language, or marketplace participation creates no implicit access.
- **Export and clean import.** The export is an Agentgres-admitted manifest with payload bytes behind artifact refs,
  exclusions with reason codes, and retention, revocation, and residual obligations; import re-runs source-rights, boundary,
  authority, integrity, privacy, retention, and canonical-owner admission in the receiving domain, possession activates
  nothing, and export permissions stay ceilings import cannot widen.
- **Revocation and declared impact action.** Revoking one source right resolves the impact graph across views, datasets,
  checkpoints, models, workers, packages, routes, exports, and commitments, and the declared action — quarantine,
  reevaluation, recall, clean reconstruction, or blocked future use — executes for each; receipts are never erased, and
  irreversibly disclosed material stays visible as residual exposure.

Standing across the chain: `Standard`/`Private` and managed/self-hosted are orthogonal, a `Private` claim needs current
custody and route evidence rather than operator identity, a toggle, or a zero-retention promise, and receipts state which
claim is supported. The canary appears only in the confidential correction and one private evaluation case — never in public
fixtures, route metadata, test names, error payloads, or receipts. Surfaces show where learning goes beside where inference
runs, and public settlement stays optional and sparse.

## Applying it in a work item

- `falsifiable_claim` names the exact boundary revision, tested route contracts, material classes, and the conformance grade
  the evidence supports; a `contract` result is not relabeled `deployment`.
- `evidence_refs` carries repo paths for the frozen benchmark and thresholds, fixture commitment, compiled-policy-hash
  reconstruction, export manifest with exclusions, and receiving-scope import receipt; the runner validates the registered
  daemon `LearningEgressReceipt` schema, not a local variant.
- `adversarial_or_fault_proof` carries run literals: pre-egress denial with packet or transport capture, seller and
  second-tenant denials with stable failure codes, the tamper battery over profile, route contract, rights claim, manifest,
  impact result, and one receipt, and the revocation impact-graph result.
- `remaining_nonclaims` records at minimum no weight-level forgetting absent verified unlearning or clean retraining, no proof
  of off-platform recipient behavior, no universal model equivalence, and no cryptographic-privacy claim for an external route.
- Missing runtime, custody, route, capture, Foundry, export, or revocation capability is blocked — never skipped or passed.

## Terminal evidence

One committed conformance report over the whole chain binds the profile ref, effective policy hash, fixture commitment, tested
route-contract and custody-proof refs, per-case verdicts with assertion receipt refs, transport-capture ref,
model-independence report ref, and impact-graph report ref. Any failed case fails the profile; a passing report proves only
the tested profile revision, routes, runtime, material classes, and evidence window, and expiry or change of any of them
requires rerun.

## Canon gaps

- "Imported into a clean deployment": canon defines import into a receiving scope, not whether a deployment with no prior
  institutional state is a distinct required case or what makes a deployment clean. Owner:
  `docs/conformance/hypervisor-core/institutional-learning-boundary.md`.
- "Frozen floors" is plural in the retained chain while canon declares one quantitative benchmark and minimum threshold;
  whether provider exit must freeze further floors, and who composes them, is unstated. Owner:
  `docs/architecture/foundations/institutional-learning-boundary.md`.
- No conformance grade is mapped to an enterprise release overlay, leaving the grade a release-facing enterprise claim demands
  unstated. Owners: `docs/conformance/hypervisor-core/institutional-learning-boundary.md` with
  `docs/architecture/_meta/execution-horizons.md`.
