The reviewer must assess the implementation against the claims above and the
assumption lines in `../specs/common_boundary_theorems.md`, not against a
stronger informal interpretation.

## Independence requirements

The final report must name the reviewer and state that the reviewer:

1. did not author the reviewed implementation or its proofs;
2. is organizationally independent of the implementation team, or clearly
   discloses any relationship that could affect independence;
3. selected their own review method and independently reproduced the tests;
4. received no instruction to suppress findings; and
5. reports all findings, including accepted or disputed findings, with stable
   identifiers and severity.

Under ADR 0049 the owner-commissioned, context-isolated Daybreak reviewer may
satisfy this owner-controlled gate if it fully discloses its automated
provenance and all findings are handled under the acceptance rules below. The
RustCrypto/fips205 interoperability oracle and the Python clean-room receipt
verifier are valuable duplicate implementations, but neither substitutes for
that reviewer. No resulting report may be described as human peer review,
institutional certification, or professional assurance.

## Required review scope

### A. Cryptographic providers and encodings

Review the pinned production provider surface and every serialization boundary:

- `dcrypt` 4.0.1 ML-DSA-44 implementation and imports in
  `crates/crypto/src/sign/dilithium/`;
- RustCrypto `slh-dsa` 0.2.0-rc.5 production verification/signing use in
  `crates/crypto/src/sign/slh_dsa.rs` and the terminal signer;
- ML-KEM-768 establishment and ChaCha20-Poly1305 record protection in
  `crates/crypto/src/transport/pq_authenticated_channel.rs`;
- canonical public-key, expanded-secret, signature, transcript, and SCALE/JCS
  decoding; malformed-input and algorithm-confusion refusal;
- dependency feature selection, randomness sources, zeroization expectations,
  and provider version/patch provenance in `Cargo.lock` and crate manifests.

The review must distinguish standards conformance/interoperability evidence
from a proof of side-channel resistance or provider correctness.

### B. Terminal-seal authority and custody

Review:

- `crates/types/src/app/consensus/seal_shares.rs`;
- `crates/validator/src/common/guardian/seal_signer.rs` and
  `crates/validator/src/common/guardian/seal_signer/state.rs`;
- `docs/decisions/0042-select-slh-dsa-for-aft-terminal-seals.md`.

Attempt to obtain a valid share through a carried/self-authenticated key,
identity substitution, configuration/epoch/domain replay, slot or root replay,
predecessor/successor rebinding, state rollback, clone, crash-window reuse,
anchor races, exhaustion, partial write, and failed erasure. Confirm that the
enrolled configuration/key-root manifest is the authority source and that
failure is closed.

The report must separately evaluate the shared-filesystem anchor's documented
deployment preconditions. It must not promote that backend to snapshot-safe
custody merely because the local crash suite passes.

### C. PQ authenticated channels and durable delivery

Review:

- `crates/crypto/src/transport/pq_authenticated_channel.rs`;
- `crates/networking/src/libp2p/pq_channel.rs`;
- the strict routing and outbox paths in `crates/networking/src/libp2p/`;
- validator configuration/rotation wiring in
  `crates/validator/src/standard/orchestration/`;
- `docs/decisions/0043-adopt-mutually-authenticated-ml-kem-aft-channels.md` and
  `docs/decisions/0047-require-payload-scoped-pq-path-evidence.md`.

Attempt unknown-key-share, identity/key substitution, cross-network,
cross-configuration and cross-account replay, downgrade, transcript splice,
role reflection, key-confirmation forgery, nonce/sequence reuse, content-type
confusion, delayed old-session delivery, outbox loss/duplication, clone
ownership, and validator-set rotation attacks. Confirm that late fallback
carriers are refused after instance retirement and cannot reactivate authority.

The payload-scoped channel proof is a graph of dual endpoint completion
attestations. The report must state whether that evidence is sufficient for the
precise T12 claim and must not reinterpret it as proof of all historical
traffic, delivery, or adaptive security.

### D. Consensus and fallback integration

Review the rooted ML-DSA vote/header authority, exact `n/f/q` geometry,
configuration-scoped timeout and fallback-start certificates, optimistic lock
handoff, hash-only fallback adapter, durable restart state, and strict
rejection of BLS/guardian evidence as a PQ consensus constituent:

- `crates/consensus/src/aft/`;
- `crates/types/src/app/consensus/{fallback,async_fallback}.rs`;
- `crates/validator/src/standard/orchestration/hash_async.rs`;
- `internal-docs/architecture/protocols/aft/specs/r10_live_tier_async_fallback.md`.

The review must explicitly check that the implementation supports only the
declared static-adversary profile and that no code or document upgrades this to
adaptive security.

### E. Evidence-derived authorization, externalization, and portable receipt boundary

Trace one demonstrated effect in causal order:

1. source-neutral runtime-v3 certificate bytes are reverified into the opaque
   guarantee type;
2. the Agentgres-committed effect and exact manifest produce the bound
   authorization consumed by the sole production external mutation owner;
3. the atomic resource consequence is recorded; and
4. that completed evidence is packaged into canonical portable receipt bytes
   and independently reverified against relying-party trust.

The portable receipt is deliberately post-consequence evidence: it contains
the execution, resource-record, outcome, and reconciliation roots. It cannot
authorize the same consequence without circularity and is not a production
mutation input. This separation is required by ADRs 0044, 0046, and 0048.

- `crates/ioi-finality/src/{runtime_v3,portable_assurance}.rs`;
- `crates/types/src/app/consensus/collapse/guarantee_vector.rs`;
- `crates/agentgres/src/consequence.rs`;
- `tools/aft-assurance-cleanroom/verify.py`;
- `.github/scripts/check_aft_production_authorization.sh`.

Attempt evidence laundering, unknown transforms, constituent omission,
classical endpoint substitution, missing channel edges, withheld terminal
shares, self-nominated network/configuration/key/signer/anchor roots,
policy/manifest/domain rebinding, collateral reuse, and duplicate or ambiguous
external invocation. Confirm that authorization requires a separately
provisioned `PortableAssuranceTrustV1` and that at-most-once externalization is
claimed only for the modeled atomic idempotency-register contract.

## Finding and disposition format

Each finding must include:

- stable ID, severity (`critical`, `high`, `medium`, `low`, or
  `informational`), affected claim and files;
- prerequisites and a reproducible trace or proof argument;
- whether it violates confidentiality, authority, safety, liveness under the
  declared model, custody, accounting, or claim accuracy;
- proposed remediation and the candidate revision retested after remediation;
- final state: `resolved`, `not-a-finding` with rationale, or `open`.

The owner and implementers may dispute severity or applicability, but disputed
items remain visible in the final report.

## Release acceptance

Gate 15 passes only when:

1. the attributable independent report names the immutable reviewed commit,
   exact automated reviewer identity/provenance, and all in-scope provider
   versions;
2. every critical and high finding is resolved and independently retested;
3. no unresolved finding invalidates a permitted claim or a release gate;
4. medium/low residuals are recorded in the implementation ledger with owners
   and bounded claim impact; and
5. any remediation after review is either inside the reviewer's retest or
   triggers a documented delta review.

Until those conditions hold, M1, M8, and the overall release remain explicitly
incomplete even if every local test passes.
