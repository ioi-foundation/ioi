## Multi-Node Does Not Mean Multi-Party

Chapter 03 ended with one institution. This chapter distributes it — and the
single most load-bearing fact is that distributing it creates **no new
parties**. One stable `system_id` may span several Hypervisor Nodes; a
Hypervisor Node names a physical or administrative deployment unit, never the
identity boundary of every system it participates in. Node membership is a
governed transition recorded in explicit membership records, and adding a node
never silently widens authority, changes quorum or finality, or proves
independence. Desired topology and observed membership remain distinct facts:
a declared three-node profile with one reachable replica is degraded, not
three-node assured. Owner:
[`governed-autonomous-systems.md`](../foundations/governed-autonomous-systems.md).

## Useful Distributed Work, Not Just Replication

Multi-node work is not merely replication. One bounded DAS may distribute
useful GoalRun roles, verification, gateway/edge work, and typed embodied
mission/action allocations across admitted members through native L0
`RuntimeAssignment`, scoped leases, state/evidence watermarks, and domain
admission. AIIP begins only where a separate constitution, operational truth,
authority, risk, and credible exit create an independently governed system
boundary.

The owner separates three composable coordination planes so "distributed" is
never one undifferentiated claim:

- **Continuity coordination** — replicas, writers, witnesses, fencing epochs,
  recovery, replay, all inside one `system_id`. It proves survival, not useful
  distribution and not independence.
- **Same-system distributed work** — heterogeneous admitted workers,
  verifiers, gateways, people, or embodied units performing real workloads
  under role topology, runtime assignments, and scoped leases. Membership makes
  a node eligible for its admitted role; it is not a work award, an authority
  grant, or permission to duplicate a consequential effect. Lease expiry,
  partitions, and reassignment reconcile before further consequential work.
- **Cross-system federation** — two or more independently governed systems.
  That is chapter 05's subject, and internal routing never becomes it by
  sharing an envelope format or transport.

A drone swarm, robot fleet, distributed facility, or multi-site service can
therefore be one bounded DAS rather than a federation of one-system-per-device.
High-frequency physical safety stays local under the two-speed boundary — the
final veto never depends on a model, cloud, or remote wallet — while
system-level planning and allocation use native membership, assignment, and
lease contracts. Owners:
[`embodied-runtime.md`](../components/daemon-runtime/embodied-runtime.md),
[`physical-action-safety.md`](../foundations/physical-action-safety.md).

## The Four Pluralities

The table below is a reader aid over one consequence of the north-star network
proof — that multiplicity is not the same as independence — and does not
define the term. The defining owner is
[`internet-of-intelligence.md`](../foundations/internet-of-intelligence.md).

IOI facilitates an Internet of Intelligence when it preserves all four forms
of plurality instead of conflating them:

| Plurality | What changes | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-model | cognition routes | independent workers or parties |
| Multi-worker | accountable labor actors | independent infrastructure or governance |
| Multi-node | placement, custody, and failure domains | independent contributors |
| Multi-party | ownership, policy, authority, attribution, dispute, economics | automatically trustworthy results |

Ten IOI-owned workers on ten nodes across several model providers remain one
party as long as one operator controls authority, truth, verification, and
settlement. That is why the first distributed proof gate lives entirely inside
one system — continuity across two failure domains plus a real workload
assigned, executed, verified, and reconciled across admitted members, with
duplicate-effect prevention and unchanged authority — before the roadmap may
claim two sovereign systems federating at all. Gate owner:
[`governed-autonomous-systems.md`](../foundations/governed-autonomous-systems.md)
with sequencing in [`execution-horizons.md`](../_meta/execution-horizons.md).
